title: Jungle Bird
ingredientTags:
  - simple syrup
  - dark rum
  - rum
  - campari
  - pineapple juice
  - lime juice
ingredients:
  - 1½ ounces Jamaican or blackstrap rum (preferably Dr. Bird)
  - 1½ ounces pineapple juice
  - ¾ ounce Campari
  - ½ ounce simple syrup (or less)
  - ½ ounce lime juice
  - Pineapple wedge (for garnish)
recipe: |
  In a mixing glass, add simple syrup, rum, Campari, pineapple juice, and lime juice. Stir until chilled. Strain into a rocks glass over one large piece of ice. Granish with pineapple wedge.
notes: |
  You can also shake the drink for some froth from the pineapple juice. You can reduce the simple syrup if you're using a less bitter bitter ;)
