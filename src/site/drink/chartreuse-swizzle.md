title: Chartreuse Swizzle
ingredientTags:
  - green chartreuse
  - velvet falernum
  - pineapple juice
  - lime juice
ingredients:
  - 1¼ ounces Green Chartreuse
  - ½ ounce Velvet Falernum
  - 1 ounce pineapple juice
  - ¾ ounce lime juice
  - Mint (for garnish)
recipe: |
  Add all liquid ingredients to shaker with ice. Shake. Strain into Collins glass over ice. Garnish with mint.
