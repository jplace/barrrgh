title: Sazerac
ingredientTags:
  - absinthe
  - rye
  - peychauds bitters
ingredients:
  - 2 sprays of absinthe (for rinse)
  - 2 ounces rye
  - 2 dashes of Peychaud's bitters
  - 1 barspoon of sugar (or more)
  - Lemon twist (for garnish)
recipe: |
  Spray absinthe into a small rocks glass. Place the glass in the freezer to chill while you make the rest of the drink.

  In a mixing glass, muddle the sugar and bitters. Add rye and ice. Stir. Strain into the chilled glass. Twist lemon peel over drink to release oils, then discard the peel.
