title: Old Fashioned
ingredientTags:
  - bourbon
  - angostura bitters
ingredients:
  - 2 ounces bourbon
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  - 2 bar spoons of "double dem" syrup (see note)
  - An orange peel
  - A brandied cherry
recipe: |
  In a rocks glass, stir together bourbon, bitters, and syrup. Add a big ice cube and a cherry. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in. Give it second to dilute before drinking.
notes: |
  To make "double dem" syrup, combine 2 parts demerara sugar and 1 part water by volume or weight. Microwave briefly to heat and stir until dissolved.

  If not using "double dem" syrup... Muddle the sugar and orange peel. Add the bourbon and stir until the sugar is dissolved: this will take ~2 minutes of passion. Then, proceed with the rest of the recipe.
